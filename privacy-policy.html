PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated June 24, 2026

This Privacy Notice for The Arc-Hives, LLC (doing business as Threshold) ("we," "us," or "our") describes how and why we might access, collect, store, use, and/or share ("process") your personal information when you use our services ("Services"), including when you:

  • Download and use our mobile application (Threshold), or any other application of ours that links to this Privacy Notice
  • Use Threshold — a weather intelligence app that provides hyperlocal severe weather monitoring, rain arrival nowcasting, and ensemble forecast scenario analysis. The app collects your device location to monitor local weather conditions, sends push notifications when severe weather risk changes at your location, and processes location data through NOAA public weather APIs. The app does not sell user data and does not display advertising.
  • Engage with us in other related ways, including any marketing or support communications

Questions or concerns? Reading this Privacy Notice will help you understand your privacy rights and choices. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services. If you have questions, please contact us at admin@thearc-hivesindex.com.

This summary provides key points from our Privacy Notice. You can find more details about any of these topics by using the table of contents below.

What personal information do we process? When you use our Services, we may process personal information depending on how you interact with us, the choices you make, and the features you use. This includes your email address, device location, and device identifiers for push notifications.

What are your rights? Depending on where you are located, applicable privacy law may give you certain rights regarding your personal information.

How do you exercise your rights? Contact us at admin@thearc-hivesindex.com. We will consider and act upon any request in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

Personal information you disclose to us

In Short: We collect personal information that you provide to us.

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on the Services or otherwise contact us. The personal information we collect may include:

  • Email addresses
  • Passwords (stored securely via Supabase authentication; we never have access to plaintext passwords)

Sensitive Information

We collect and process your precise device location when you use the Services. This location data is used solely to monitor weather conditions and severe weather risk at your chosen locations. You may add locations manually without granting device location access. If you wish to change our access to your device location, you may do so in your device settings.

Application Data

If you use our application, we also collect the following information if you provide us with access or permission:

  • Mobile Device Location. We request access to your device's location to determine your current position for weather monitoring. You may grant or deny this permission at any time in your device settings. Location data is not stored persistently — it is used only at the moment of your request to fetch weather data for your coordinates.
  • Push Notifications. We request permission to send you push notifications regarding severe weather changes at your monitored locations. If you wish to opt out, you may turn off notifications in your device settings at any time.
  • Device Identifiers. We collect device push token identifiers (via Expo Push Notifications) to deliver weather alerts to your device. These identifiers are stored in our database and associated with your account solely for the purpose of sending notifications.

All personal information you provide must be true, complete, and accurate. Please notify us of any changes to your personal information.

2. HOW DO WE PROCESS YOUR INFORMATION?

In Short: We process your information to provide, improve, and administer our Services and to send weather alerts.

We process your personal information for the following reasons:

  • To facilitate account creation and authentication. We process your email and password to create and maintain your account.
  • To deliver weather monitoring services. We use your saved location coordinates to fetch weather data from NOAA public APIs and present it in the app.
  • To send severe weather push notifications. We poll the SPC (Storm Prediction Center) every 15 minutes and send push notifications to your registered device when severe weather risk escalates or de-escalates at your monitored locations.
  • To respond to user inquiries and provide support. We may process your information to respond to your questions or resolve issues with the Services.
  • To send administrative information. We may process your information to send details about changes to our terms or policies.
  • To protect our Services. We may process your information as part of our efforts to keep the Services safe and secure.
  • To evaluate and improve our Services. We may process your information to identify usage trends and improve the app experience.
  • To comply with our legal obligations. We may process your information to comply with applicable law and defend our legal rights.

3. WHEN AND WITH WHOM DO WE SHARE YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION?

In Short: We may share information in specific situations with the following third parties.

We may need to share your personal information in the following situations:

  • Business Transfers. We may share or transfer your information in connection with any merger, sale of company assets, financing, or acquisition of all or a portion of our business.
  • Service Providers. We share information with the following service providers who assist us in operating the Services:
    • Supabase — authentication and database hosting
    • Railway — backend API and background worker hosting
    • Vercel — web frontend hosting
    • Expo / Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — push notification delivery
    • RevenueCat / Google Play Billing — subscription and payment management
    • Anthropic — plain-language synthesis of weather data (your location coordinates and weather readings are sent to Anthropic's API to generate natural-language nowcast and forecast summaries; no personally identifiable information is included in these requests)

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Weather data is fetched from NOAA public APIs (SPC, NWS, MRMS, AIGEFS) and is not user data — it is publicly available weather information retrieved on your behalf.

8. CONTROLS FOR DO-NOT-TRACK FEATURES

Most web browsers and some mobile operating systems include a Do-Not-Track ("DNT") feature or setting you can activate to signal your privacy preference not to have data about your online browsing activities monitored and collected. As no uniform technology standard for recognizing and implementing DNT signals has been finalized, we do not currently respond to DNT signals. If a standard for online tracking is adopted that we must follow in the future, we will inform you about that practice in a revised version of this Privacy Notice.
