Delete Your Threshold Account

The Arc-Hives, LLC — Threshold: Weather Intelligence

You can request deletion of your Threshold account and all associated data at any time by contacting us directly.

How to Request Account Deletion

Step 1: Send an email to admin@thearc-hivesindex.com

Subject: Account Deletion Request

Include: The email address associated with your Threshold account

We will process your request within 30 days and send a confirmation email when complete.

What Gets Deleted

Upon account deletion, the following data is permanently removed:

  • Your account credentials (email address and password)
  • All saved locations and weather monitoring preferences
  • Push notification subscriptions
  • Subscription and billing records stored in our system
  • All cached weather data associated with your account

What May Be Retained

The following data may be retained after account deletion:

  • Transaction records — Google Play manages payment history and may retain billing records in accordance with their own policies and applicable law
  • Legal compliance records — We may retain minimal records as required by applicable law for up to 7 years

Cancel Your Subscription First

If you have an active Threshold subscription, please cancel it through Google Play before requesting account deletion to avoid future charges. Go to Google Play → Subscriptions → Threshold → Cancel.
